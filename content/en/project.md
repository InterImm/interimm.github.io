InterImm imagines the Interplanetary Immigration Center, an institution that helps people build a life on Mars, and builds its world with real science: Mars time, signal delay between the planets, transfer orbits and city plans. Everywhere else on this site speaks as the Center. This page speaks for the project.

Everything here is open. The stories, the archive, the calculators and this website are public repositories in the [InterImm organization on GitHub](https://github.com/InterImm). Anyone can propose a building in a Martian city, correct a calculation or write a chapter of [the book](https://book.interimm.org/).

## How it started

The project grew out of the InterImm Round Table, twenty online discussions about interplanetary civilization held between 2014 and 2018. Their notes are still on [GitHub](https://github.com/InterImm/roundTable/issues/4).
